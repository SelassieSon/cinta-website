As a species, we are gifted with two thinking systems.

In simple terms

System 1 thinking is considered to be our ancient way of sensing the world that we live in ([#emotions](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=emotions&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6796117257006198784) , [#intuition](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=intuition&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6796117257006198784) , [#motivation](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=motivation&highlightedUpdateUrns=urn%3Ali%3Aactivity%3A6796117257006198784) ).

System 2 is how we have been able to develop things like the scientific method and thinking tools like a SWOT/PEST/Wardly Maps (use of logic) to fact check our System 1 process.

System 1 is filled with biases for good reason.

Listen to [Rob Jardine](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ACoAAAV6uVsBoLE4CefZMv7IW-XyIm2Sv6gMv7I) & Rob Pearson as they explain the pros/cons and how to manage your own mind.
