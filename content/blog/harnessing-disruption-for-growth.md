As we close out2024, businesses continue to face massive disruption, from the ongoing global economic slowdown to the impact of AI tools like ChatGPT and MidJourney revolutionizing industries. In fact, a recent report highlighted that 70% of companies are now investing in AI to enhance decision-making and streamline operations, leading to a projected $15.7 trillion contribution to the global economy by 2030 [PwC AI Report](https://www.pwc.com/gx/en/issues/analytics/assets/pwc-ai-analysis-sizing-the-prize-report.pdf)

At CINTA & Co., we see disruption as an opportunity for growth. The recent AI-driven surge in workplace automation and the ongoing shift towards hybrid work environments [Microsoft’s 2024 Work Trend Index](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/worklab/work-trend-index) have demonstrated that organizations willing to adapt quickly are better positioned for long-term success.

Organizational plasticity—the ability to adapt, innovate, and evolve—remains key. Those that cultivate resilient leadership and leverage AI to drive strategic decisions are reaping the rewards.

In light of the recent regulatory debates surrounding AI, such as the EU’s AI Act [Euractiv report](https://www.euractiv.com/section/digital/news/eu-ai-act/), businesses must prepare to navigate these changes while staying agile. How can your organization turn these disruptions into opportunities for growth?

By fostering a culture of adaptability, embedding AI tools, and driving resilient leadership, you can thrive in this evolving landscape.
