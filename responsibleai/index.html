Regulatory landscape

The compliance environment is not waiting for you

These frameworks govern Ontario organizations. Each has AI implications, and most are already in force.

EDSTA

Enhancing Digital Security and Trust Act

In force since January 2025. Covers FIPPA and MFIPPA institutions, school boards, and children's aid societies.

IPC-OHRC

Principles for Responsible Use of AI

Published January 2026. Soft law, but the de facto assessment standard for the broader public sector.

FIPPA / MFIPPA

Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy

In force. Increasingly relevant as AI systems process and decide using personal information.

PHIPA

Personal Health Information Protection Act

In force. Triggered by AI for intake, matching, and clinical decision support.
