Our story

The thesis has not changed. The urgency has.

CINTA & Co. was founded in 2020 on a simple premise. Mid-market organizations need a different kind of consulting partner. Not a Big Four firm that assigns junior analysts, and not a generalist freelancer. A firm that understands their scale, their constraints, and their ambition.

In 2020, the challenge was digital transformation broadly. In 2026, it is compounding disruption. AI adoption, regulatory complexity, workforce change, economic pressure, and demographic shifts arrive together, not one at a time.

You do not need an AI company or a training vendor. You need a transformation partner who understands all of these forces and can help you respond coherently. That is what we do.
